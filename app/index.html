    Where every fact came from

    Every trophy's guide names the guide or database its facts were checked against. Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as the PlayStation 5 list publishes them; nothing here is copied from another guide.

    How this tracker works

    • Ticks save to this browser's localStorage — no account, no network.
    • Export writes leonida-progress.json; Import reads it back, so progress survives a new browser.
    • The spoiler switch hides the names and descriptions of story and secret trophies until you mark them earned. Earned trophies always show.
    • Filters: kind, grade, missable only, hide earned, plus a full-text search across descriptions, guides and locations.
    • Views have addresses — #/board, #/board/cunning-stunts, #/roadmap — and ?demo=1 opens a read-only mid-run save for screenshots.
