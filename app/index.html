    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    Seven stages. Everything in stages 2 to 6 survives the credits — the story is the only part with a clock on it, and only one meditation inside it can actually be lost. About 200 hours end to end, most of it the 100% checklist.

      What can actually be lost

      One trophy. That is the honest answer: out of 55, only Positive Mental Attitude has a window that closes, and it closes at the end of Chapter 1. The rest of this page explains why the usual suspects are not missable, so you can stop worrying about them.
